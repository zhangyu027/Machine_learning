## Included Visuals

| Figure | Purpose |
|---|---|
| cspp_enrollment_growth.jpg | Historical and predicted statewide enrollment trend |

| top_10_vendor_growth_forecast.jpg | Top vendor enrollment growth forecast |

## Suggested Presentation Order

1. Start with statewide enrollment trend.
2. Show county contribution map.
3. Show top site heatmap.
4. Show top site/vendor contribution.
5. End with forecast enrollment and growth charts.
