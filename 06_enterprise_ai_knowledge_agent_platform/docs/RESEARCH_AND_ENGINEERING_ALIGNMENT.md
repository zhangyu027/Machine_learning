# Research and Engineering Alignment

This project demonstrates enterprise GenAI platform patterns:

- RAG over private documents
- structured SQL analytics
- multi-tool orchestration
- source-grounded generation
- hallucination risk checks
- confidence scoring
- local/private deployment

For healthcare and public-sector data, enterprise AI systems need privacy controls, PII avoidance, evidence citations, data lineage, de-identification, human review, and auditability.
